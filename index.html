Dispatch

An independent iPhone app for working with Devin from your phone.

Dispatch is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Cognition AI. Devin is a product of Cognition AI. You need your own Devin account to use Dispatch.

Getting started

  • Open Devin on the web and go to Settings → Devin API.
  • Create a personal access token (recommended) or an API key, and copy it.
  • Open Dispatch, paste the token, and tap Connect.

Your token is stored in the iPhone Keychain and is only ever sent to Devin (api.devin.ai and mcp.devin.ai).

Common questions

“Needs a newer token.” Schedules, reviews, scans, knowledge and insights use Devin's newer API. Connect with a personal access token instead of a legacy API key.

Notifications arrive late. Dispatch checks Devin from your phone in the background, so iOS decides when that happens. Keep Background App Refresh on for Dispatch, and don't force-quit the app.

A session says “Exceeded limit”. Devin paused it when a usage limit was reached. Once your quota has reset, send a message to resume it.

More than one organization. A Devin token belongs to one organization. Add one account per organization in Settings → Accounts and switch between them.

Removing your data. Settings → Disconnect deletes your token from the phone. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.
